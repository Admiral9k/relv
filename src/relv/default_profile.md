# Profile

## Who I am

A hands-on builder and tinkerer running a personal stack of local and
self-hosted AI tools. I live in the terminal and in markdown; my notes and
pipelines are plain-text-first. I'll steal a good idea from anywhere, but I
triage hard — the firehose is infinite and my attention isn't.

## What I run

- Local AI agents for research, coding, and document processing
- Multi-model routing through OpenRouter plus small local models
- An ingest pipeline: markdown in, structured queues out
- Self-hosted services; SQLite over managed databases; cheap beats fancy

## What I value

- Small, single-purpose tools that do one thing well (I can compose them)
- Open-source and self-hostable; BYO-key over per-seat subscriptions
- Mechanisms I can port into my own projects, not just products to buy
- Agents and pipelines with structured in/out — prose is for humans, data is
  for machines

## What I reject

- Marketing-first SaaS with a thin AI wrapper and per-seat pricing
- Enterprise listicles, press-release noise, "top 10 tools" blog spam
- Tools that don't work offline or that lock my data in someone else's cloud
- anything that demands a new account before showing me what it does
